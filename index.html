Our mission

The MLDR Foundation exists to create positive impact through drone racing, technology, aviation, education, and community engagement. Our mission is to inspire, educate, empower, and create opportunities for communities through sports entertainment and innovation.

Drone racing is a fast, exciting sport that naturally teaches physics, engineering, electronics, coding and teamwork. We use that excitement to bring hands-on learning to classrooms, give young pilots a pathway into aviation and technology, and bring first responders and neighbors together at community events that raise money for local schools and causes.

Your gift funds drone equipment, curriculum, training, scholarships and event access for students. You can give online in under a minute:
