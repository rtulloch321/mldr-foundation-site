Our impact

Real experiences that put drones, engineering and aviation in students’ hands.

Our impact so far

  • ~30Events held
  • ~150People trained
  • 6Years operating

What these numbers mean

  • About 30 events held: drone racing competitions, school demonstrations, community and charity events over six years.
  • About 150 people trained: students, young pilots and community members who received hands-on drone flight and safety training.
  • 6 years operating: six years of MLDR drone racing and education programming.

Where donations go

Donations are directed to program costs: drone and safety equipment, curriculum and training, scholarships, and access to events for students and schools. Campaign gifts for a specific school go to that school’s STEM, aviation and student programs.

Current campaign

Support Legacy Traditional Schools – Laveen and Legacy High School Las Vegas. Your gift helps bring hands-on drone racing, flight and engineering experiences into the classroom, fund aviation exposure and career pathways, and open student programs that build skills, confidence and opportunity.
