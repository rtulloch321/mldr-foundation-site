Our programs

Five program areas that use drone racing to teach, connect and give back.

Minor League Drone Racing

Develops youth and amateur drone racing talent and creates pathways into future opportunities in aviation and technology.

Young pilots learn to fly safely, build and maintain their drones, and compete as a team. Training builds hand-eye coordination, problem-solving and sportsmanship, and the best-prepared students can move into higher levels of competition and scholarship opportunities.

  • Youth pilot development
  • Training programs
  • Competitions
  • Scholarships

MLDR 1st Responders Series™

Brings together police, fire, EMS, veterans, and community members through positive engagement and fundraising events.

First responders and veterans fly alongside community members in friendly charity races. The events build trust between public-safety teams and the neighborhoods they serve, and showcase how drone technology supports search, rescue and emergency response.

  • Charity races
  • Team-building events
  • Community outreach
  • Fundraising initiatives

STEM & Education

Introduces students to aviation, technology, engineering, and future careers through hands-on learning.

We bring drone flight, engineering and racing experiences into classrooms and school events. Students see physics, electronics, coding and teamwork in action, and meet professionals who use drones and aviation in their careers.

  • School programs
  • Drone education
  • Workshops
  • Career days
  • Hands-on learning

Scholarships & Community Impact

Provides opportunities and support to students, individuals, and community organizations.

Scholarships and small grants help students and community groups access equipment, training and education that would otherwise be out of reach, with a focus on widening access to technology for young people.

  • Student scholarships
  • Community grants
  • Youth initiatives
  • Technology access programs

DroneRace To FundRaise™

Signature charity events that raise money and awareness for schools and important community causes.

A DroneRace To FundRaise event turns a live drone race into a fundraiser for a partner school or cause. Current campaigns support the STEM, aviation and student programs at Legacy Traditional Schools – Laveen (Arizona) and Legacy High School Las Vegas (Nevada), funding equipment, curriculum, event access and student opportunities.

  • Charity partnerships
  • Fundraising campaigns
  • Community engagement
  • Sponsor participation
